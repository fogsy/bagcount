# Google Play listing draft

## App name
BoxingAI

## Short description
AI boxing coach: throw punches on camera, get scored, climb your city league.

## Full description
BoxingAI turns your phone into a pad man. Front-camera pose tracking counts jabs, crosses, hooks and uppercuts on-device — no bag required.

Train daily missions, run drills with a voice coach, build streaks and XP, and climb weekly city / friends / global leaderboards.

Premium unlocks the full drill library and 30-day fight-camp plan. Core Pad Cam tracking stays free.
